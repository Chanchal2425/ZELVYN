The Challenge

The agency was managing client relationships and deals across three separate Google Sheets, Slack channels, and email inboxes. As the team grew, deal statuses became desynchronized, client renewals were forgotten, and calculating monthly sales rep commissions required 2 full days of manual accounting calculation.

Our Architectural Approach

We designed a dedicated web application with a lightning-fast relational PostgreSQL backend. The user interface was built cleanly without unnecessary external framework overhead, ensuring sub-second response times even with thousands of historical customer records.

Key Features Engineered

  • Visual Kanban Board: Drag-and-drop deal management across Discovery, Scoping, Proposal Sent, and Closed Won.
  • Automated Commission Rules: Dynamically calculates sales rep splits based on deal margins and milestone payments.
  • Stale Deal Reminders: Triggers notifications to sales reps whenever a deal remains in a stage without activity for over 5 business days.

Technology Stack

FastAPI / Python PostgreSQL / Supabase Vanilla JavaScript Chart.js Tailwind CSS (Custom Minified)

Engineered Outcome

The bespoke CRM unified the sales process into a single source of truth. Reps log activities in seconds, leadership has an instant visual summary of expected monthly cash flows, and zero monthly SaaS seat licensing fees are incurred.
