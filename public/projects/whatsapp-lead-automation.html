The Challenge

Real estate agencies and high-ticket service companies invest heavily in Click-to-WhatsApp Meta ads. However, when inquiries arrive outside business hours or during high-volume campaigns, leads often wait 3 to 6 hours before a human representative responds. In that delay, prospective buyers look at competing listings or lose interest, resulting in wasted ad spend and low appointment conversion.

Our Architectural Approach

Rather than relying on basic auto-replies or generic rigid menu trees, we built a webhook-driven state machine on the official Meta WhatsApp Cloud API. The system manages conversational memory, asks clarifying questions dynamically, presents interactive list pickers, and validates contact availability against live calendar slots.

Key Features Engineered

  • Sub-5-Second Greeting: Welcome messages tailored to the specific ad campaign the user clicked.
  • Interactive Lead Qualification: Budget range selectors, location preference chips, and buyer intent scoring.
  • Live Calendar Sync: Checks available consultant slots and books meetings directly with automated Google Meet links.
  • Interactive Lead Management: Directly assigns qualified leads to the appropriate representatives.

Technology Stack

Meta WhatsApp Cloud API AIsensy Google Calendar API Automated Workflows

Engineered Outcome

The system completely eliminates the manual friction of initial lead screening and scheduling. Inbound inquiries receive instant, context-aware responses 24/7, high-intent prospects secure confirmed calendar bookings without phone tag, and sales teams receive enriched lead profiles before every consultation.
